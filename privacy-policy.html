🚩 RedFlag - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: RedFlag collects no data. None.

Everything runs on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no servers that receive your information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (XP, level, badges, statistics, streaks and settings) is stored locally in your browser or app storage so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device, and you can erase it at any time in Settings → Reset progress or by clearing the app's data.

“Check a Message”

Text you paste into the message checker is analysed entirely on your device by built-in rules. It is not uploaded, stored, logged or shared. Links in pasted text are never opened or contacted.

Sharing

If you choose to share your score card or a challenge link, your device's own share sheet is used and you decide where it goes. A challenge link contains only a random game seed and a score - no personal information.

Permissions

RedFlag does not request access to your contacts, messages, location, camera, microphone or files. Optional features such as vibration and read-aloud use your device's built-in capabilities and send nothing anywhere.

Children

RedFlag is designed to be suitable for families. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Fictional content

All companies, banks, couriers, apps, websites, people and phone numbers in the game are fictional and used for education only.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions? Please open an issue on the project's repository.
